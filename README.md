## Project Areas

### Security, Risk & Controls

Developing practical approaches for reviewing small-business systems, access, administrative privileges, security settings, operational controls, documentation, and remediation needs.

The emphasis is on making security work understandable and actionable: identify the risk, document the evidence, define the control, assign remediation, and verify the result.

### Business Systems & Financial Controls

Applying controls thinking across accounting, payroll, CRM, Microsoft 365, and other operational platforms. This includes access review, workflow analysis, exception handling, process documentation, audit readiness, and remediation of weak or inconsistent controls.

### Data Quality & Automation

Building repeatable tools and workflows for deduplication, fuzzy matching, normalization, validation, exception reporting, structured-data processing, and controlled human review.

Current work uses Python, pandas, SQL, Excel, CSV/JSON, and schema-driven validation to turn inconsistent operational data into reliable inputs for business and security decisions.

### Structured AI Workflows

Designing AI-assisted workflows around defined schemas, validation, evidence requirements, confidence handling, and human review rather than ad hoc prompting.

The goal is to use AI as part of a reproducible system, not as a substitute for controls, verification, or judgment.

## Background

I have founder-level experience spanning **business operations, accounting and financial systems, internal controls, client service, process improvement, technology selection, and administration**.

Rather than treating that background as separate from cybersecurity, I use it as domain knowledge. Security problems often surface inside ordinary business systems: an unnecessary administrator account, an undocumented process, weak segregation of duties, inconsistent data, a missing approval step, poor evidence retention, or a control that exists on paper but not in practice.

That is the space I am increasingly focused on: **where technical systems, organizational risk, controls, and real business operations meet**.
